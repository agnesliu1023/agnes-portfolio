Hey, I’m Agnes.

A graphic designer working across branding, visual identity and user experience — between digital and physical, and between the U.S. and China.

I like building complete visual systems: logos and typography, interfaces, campaigns and brand guidelines. The work I’m drawn to has to be both strategic and visually clear, and it usually needs storytelling and practical problem-solving in the same breath.

I studied Graphic Design at the Rhode Island School of Design, taught as a RISD Museum Spalter Teaching Fellow, and interned twice at Publicis Groupe. Now I’m a contract AI Design Specialist at SpaceX.
