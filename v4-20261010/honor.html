HONOR Magic V5 Launch

Launch key visuals, social posters and short-form motion concepts for HONOR’s flagship foldable — built so its thinness and strength read at a glance.

01

Feature posters, one selling point each

The brief was to translate the Magic V5’s key message — strength and premium build — into a high-impact key visual for global social placements and fast scanning.

Each poster isolates a single selling point, so someone scrolling gets the claim before they read the copy. I delivered the KV master files plus social-ready exports for Instagram feed and story, with platform-safe margins.

02

Built for global distribution

To support the overseas social rollout, every master was rebuilt in two format variations — a 4:5 feed post and a 9:16 story — keeping hierarchy and brand cues identical, so each platform got a native crop without losing consistency.

03

Social video: TapTap

A short-form, mobile-first film introducing the Magic V5’s TapTap feature for global social platforms. It needed a strong first-second hook, clear benefit-led messaging and a clean end card that could roll out across markets.

04

“One More Thin”

HONOR asked for a short-form video set that communicates the Magic V5’s ultra-thin design simply and intuitively for global audiences — moving beyond technical specs to everyday comparisons.

We developed the “One More Thin” concept: the phone sits beside familiar thin things — a pen, a pour of milk, a shelf of CD cases — and the comparison does the talking.

05

Storyboard

I structured the storyboard and visual composition around clean side-profile shots and minimal lifestyle settings, so the message is understood within seconds while keeping a refined, premium tone for social rollout.
