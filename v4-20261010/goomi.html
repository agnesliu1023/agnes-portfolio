The brief

Goomi Academy, an online education platform, asked for a clear, modern identity that is recognizable, flexible and easy to apply across social, product, website, packaging and print.

I led the visual design from start to end, client-facing — concept exploration, system building and asset delivery — then art directed the promotional film and contributed to the interactive website.

02

Concept development

I brainstormed and sketched the first directions, then spent much of the project researching, meeting stakeholders and revising ideas. The real challenge was moving from a wordmark to a mascot — and the animal itself evolved through several iterations before the penguin stuck.

03

Logo

The final mark pairs a friendly penguin with a rounded wordmark — warm enough for students and parents, simple enough to hold up at icon size. It comes as landscape and portrait lockups with clear-space rules, set in Basenji Variable.

04

A character with expressions

To give the brand a voice, the penguin got a set of expressions and stickers — cheering, sleeping, celebrating — that carry one personality across chat, social and merchandise.

05

From flat mark to 3D character

For the promotional film I adapted the mascot into a 3D character, so the logo itself could carry the story from scene to scene.

I worked with the Goomi team on the film’s visual narrative — storyboard ideation, scene development, colour treatment and refinement — so the final video communicates Goomi’s playful, immersive and future-facing world.

06

One penguin across every screen

The same penguin then held the product together — website, social profile and stories — so every touchpoint felt like one place to learn.

07

Launch

At Goomi’s Boston launch event I presented the logo concept and the full identity system behind it, alongside the event graphics, backdrop and merchandise.
