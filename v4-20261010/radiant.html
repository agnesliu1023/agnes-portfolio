Radiant

A French-fold magazine about the duality of happiness — curated joy on the outside, loneliness and pressure hidden inside the fold.

01

Overview

This magazine explores the duality of happiness through a French-fold binding. In the context of social media and college life, the fold separates two layers: the outside shows curated happiness — the self made visible to others — while the hidden interior holds loneliness, pressure and emotional disconnection.

02

Magazine cover

The dual-image cover reinforces the split, presenting happiness as something both performed and questioned. Through folding, concealment and reveal, the book makes the unstable boundary between appearance and reality physical.

03

Inside the fold

Each spread hides a second page inside the fold — what’s shown and what’s held back, bound together in one object.

04

Website

The magazine extends into a website that carries the same tension between surface and interior.
