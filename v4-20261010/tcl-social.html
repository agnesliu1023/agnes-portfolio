TCL Social Posters

Single-message product posters, motion posters and AI-assisted films for TCL’s overseas Instagram and Facebook — each asset built around one key selling point.

SocialAIGCMotion poster
Brief, my role, process and deliverables
Launched2025 · Overseas social
Client

Markets
US, Europe, Italy
Platforms
Instagram, Facebook
01

Washing machine, made with AI video

We needed a product-focused social post for TCL’s P5 washer, highlighting autoStain Removal and the 540 mm drum for stronger rinsing and cleaner results. We delivered a social-ready static poster with headline and post copy, packaged in Instagram and Facebook formats for the overseas rollout.

02

Storyboard

We first built the main character assets — facial close-ups and full-body shots — to keep the actor consistent across frames. After confirming the interior style with a moodboard and references, I designed the storyboard and used it, together with detailed prompts, to generate each scene and motion beat. I then selected the strongest outputs, refined key details and assembled the final social-ready cut.

03

Mega Space, in motion

A static social poster and a motion poster for TCL’s Mega Space refrigerator, focused on one message: the large storage drawer, shown easily fitting an XL item. We gathered references and sketched to lock the composition, then assembled the final in Photoshop — product imagery, typography and AI-generated components refined into one cohesive layout.

04

Further development: freshness

We strengthened the message by expanding beyond the storage drawer into a second key selling point — freshness — and developed a follow-up social video. Through storyboarding and an AI-assisted approach we told a short story of food staying fresh inside the refrigerator, then delivered the static post and video assets for publishing.

05

Static posters

TCL also asked for ongoing static posts to build brand awareness. We kept the same rhythm — quick sketches and multiple options, an internal review, alignment with the client on direction — then refined the selected versions and delivered them for final approval before posting.
