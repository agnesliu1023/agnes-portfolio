People’s Republic of Involution

A speculative nation brand that turns a culture of endless competition into its own Olympics — expressed through branding, illustration and editorial design.

01

Welcome to PRI

A nation brand designed to visualize the cost of endless competition. In a society driven by productivity and comparison, people are pushed into a constant rat race — working harder for marginal gains while only a small fraction benefits. The pressure is not only economic; it becomes cultural, emotional and even parental.

02

The PRI Olympics

After observing involution in Chinese cities — housing pressure, the education arms race, “996” work culture and status-driven consumption — I reframed these forces as a system rather than individual failure.

I chose the Olympics as PRI’s core theme because it turns everyday pressure into public spectacle: rules, rankings and “winning” become the language of survival. In the poster series, each discipline satirizes a different form of endless competition — mothers of the PRI compete in cycling, chasing “parental care” statistics that are above average, as if parenting were a race.

03

Rat race: the national mark

The brand mark merges a double-headed hammer into an infinity-like loop, with both ends straining toward a single nail at the centre. It visualizes involution as a self-reinforcing cycle — constant pressure, ranking anxiety and optimization that feels intense yet produces little progress. With the nail inside the loop, the mark suggests a system that keeps striking without moving forward, inviting audiences to see the rat race as a designed structure we can recognize, question and break.

04

Make the invisible system visible

A custom typeface anchors PRI’s identity in the same logic as the mark — hammer, nail and the loop of endless optimization. The letterforms are built from rigid, tool-like strokes that feel engineered rather than expressive: straight verticals read like nails, the status quo, while clipped terminals and angled cuts echo a hammer strike. Embedded in the alphabet, the ideology becomes infrastructure — present in every label, form field, headline and instruction.

05

Visual identity

To make PRI believable as an institution, I set the typeface inside an official frame of tight grids, forms, codes and index panels with propaganda-like clarity. Two governing lines act as a bureaucratic baseline, the hammer mark sits locked at the centre — stuck in the system it enforces — and the infinite loop becomes a fifth brand element. Together they unify PRI’s passport, dossier pages and entry forms into one nation-brand ecosystem.

06

From object to identity mark

I began by building a double-headed hammer sculpture to embody involution — one side driving the nail in, the other pulling it out. That physical form became the reference for the mark. Through iteration I kept the core structure while refining it into a simpler, more scalable symbol that better captures the loop and works for institutional use.
