Overview

The 45th anniversary of the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone was the launchpad for a global-ready bilingual visual system. The brief: key visuals, posters and landing pages that match in look and message across domestic and overseas channels — and a new overseas Instagram presence, launched and managed.

01

Global brand system

An overseas-ready identity for Shenzhen Culture & Tourism that works alongside the existing domestic logo: bilingual Chinese–English typography rules and core components — logos, poster frameworks and layout tokens — scaled into a full campaign toolkit.

Overseas account launch

To reach beyond a China-first audience, we launched and standardized new accounts on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. I turned the identity into platform-ready templates — profile, headers, thumbnails and post formats — so the brand rolled out consistently from day one.

02

SEZ 45th birthday drone and light show

The system’s biggest moment: the Special Economic Zone’s 45th-anniversary show on 26 August 2025, where 12,000 drones flew at once. I created the key visual and bilingual assets, rolled out across social platforms with tailored crops — core posters in Chinese and English, “History of Shenzhen” timeline posters, a countdown series and event visuals.

Campaign ecosystem

The master key visual and custom type scaled across overseas social, WeChat and offline placements in Shenzhen — event-intro backgrounds, poster variants and a WeChat Mini Program for event information — keeping promotion consistent before and during the show.

Drone design for the logo

Letterforms became icons unique to Shenzhen — a letter turned into an arrow or a wave to evoke mountains, modern architecture and the sea — so the logotype read as the city in the night sky.

Countdown posters

The countdown built momentum with bold, time-led headlines and subtle landmark cues. One bilingual layout — the same hierarchy and spacing in Chinese and English — and a display type derived from the brand font kept the series together.

Typography

We developed three bilingual typeface options, presented them to the client, then finalized the chosen system so Chinese and English stay consistent on every poster.

03

Global tourism theme film

The official theme film frames the city through four pillars — nature, future, science and technology, and vitality — and invites global audiences to experience Shenzhen beyond the screen.

Key visual shoot direction

For the key-visual system we wrote a shooting plan around the city’s four temperaments: hero locations and framing that composite into a collage-style city — wide establishing shots, high and low architectural angles, and details such as textures, signage, sculptures and tech exhibits that work as modular cutouts beside the brand’s geometric colour graphics.

“Amazing Shenzhen!” slogan mark

The city’s official global tourism theme song needed a slogan mark. I designed the core wordmark — custom lettering in black and in the theme gradient.

Logo usage

For the global rollout the slogan became a modular lockup that sits with the legacy Chinese logo or the new English logo, with the same gradient in every pairing.
